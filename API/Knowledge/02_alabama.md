## Alabama: Valent Partners coverage offered

Valent Partners' prototype offers Homeowners, Flood, Jewelry, and Auto insurance in Alabama, alone or bundled in one application. Alabama is one of the three sprint demo states, with Texas and New York; carrier availability has not been verified yet. Estimates on the website are demo ranges adjusted for Alabama (see "Alabama estimate adjustments" below). Alabama ZIP codes start with 350 through 369. The state insurance regulator is the Alabama Department of Insurance (aldoi.gov).

## Alabama auto insurance: minimum liability required

Alabama requires minimum auto liability coverage of 25/50/25: $25,000 for bodily injury to one person, $50,000 for bodily injury per accident, and $25,000 for property damage. These are legal minimums; many drivers choose higher limits. Source: Alabama Department of Revenue, Mandatory Liability Insurance (revenue.alabama.gov/tax-types/mandatory-liability-insurance).

## Alabama auto insurance: PIP and uninsured motorist coverage

Personal injury protection (PIP) is not required by Alabama law. Uninsured motorist (UM) coverage must be offered by the insurance company, and the driver can turn it down in writing (needs verification with the Alabama Department of Insurance). Collision and comprehensive are optional under state law, but lenders usually require them on financed or leased vehicles.

## Alabama homeowners insurance: hurricanes and wind deductibles

The main weather risk for Alabama homes is hurricanes and named storms, especially in coastal Baldwin and Mobile counties. Coastal Alabama policies often have a separate hurricane or named-storm deductible, set as a percentage of the dwelling (Coverage A) amount instead of a flat dollar amount (needs verification). A percentage deductible means a 2% deductible on a $300,000 dwelling is $6,000.

## Alabama homeowners insurance: the Beach Pool (insurer of last resort)

If private insurers won't cover a coastal home, the Alabama Insurance Underwriting Association (AIUA), also called the FAIR Plan or the "Beach Pool," covers eligible homes in coastal Baldwin and Mobile counties (needs verification of current eligibility). It is a last-resort option, not a regular insurance company choice.

## Alabama homeowners insurance: FORTIFIED discounts for storm-resistant homes

Alabama insurers must give wind-premium discounts for homes built or retrofitted to the IBHS FORTIFIED standard, which strengthens roofs and other parts of a home against high winds. The source is a 2014 Alabama Department of Insurance handout on windstorm mitigation discounts (aldoi.gov/PDF/Legal/HandoutWindstormDamageMitigation.pdf); the current discount percentages need verification.

## Alabama flood insurance

Flood insurance rules in Alabama are the federal NFIP rules, the same as in every state: building coverage commonly up to $250,000, contents up to $100,000, separate deductibles, and usually a 30-day waiting period. Homeowners policies and the Beach Pool do not cover flooding. On the Alabama coast, hurricane wind and hurricane flooding (storm surge) are covered by different policies, so coastal homes usually need both a wind policy and a flood policy (needs verification).

## Alabama jewelry insurance

No Alabama-specific rules for jewelry insurance were found in Valent Partners' research (needs verification). Jewelry coverage limits, scheduling, and deductibles come from the insurance company's policy, not the state.

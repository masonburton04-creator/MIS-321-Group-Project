Assumptions, Metric Definitions & Open Questions

Required end-of-build output for client review. Nothing in this section is a finalized business rule.

  • Sessions started — mock count of users entering the funnel at Landing.
  • Completion rate — mock % of sessions reaching Completion.
  • Abandonment rate — 100% minus completion rate.
  • Avg. session time — sum of mock average dwell time across steps; not a measured production metric yet.
  • Quick-exit rate — illustrative average of per-step "left very soon after arriving" rates.
  • Dwell time — illustrative average time on a step; distinct from hover/focus time (see clarification banner above).
  • Validation-error rate — illustrative % of step visits that triggered a form validation error.
  • Needs Attention — a mock composite of exit rate, quick-exit rate, and validation-error rate, flagged only to demonstrate the concept.

  • "Needs Attention" is shown above an illustrative friction-score threshold in this prototype only.
  • No specific "quick exit" time boundary (e.g., "< 10 seconds") is asserted as final — a placeholder concept only.
  • State and insurance-line multipliers used to vary mock data were invented for demonstration purposes and are not based on real Valent Partners or carrier data.

  • Exact production event names, timestamps, and metric formulas.
  • Which metrics belong on the landing dashboard vs. deeper drill-down pages.
  • Employee vs. admin role permissions for reviewer access.
  • Retention, privacy, and consent requirements for behavioral tracking.
  • Which applicant-level details a reviewer should be able to open from aggregate analytics.

  • When you say "hover" or "how long users stay," do you mean page time, field-focus time, actual mouse-hover duration, or something else?
  • What should count as leaving "quickly" — a fixed number of seconds, leaving before interacting, or something else?
  • Which application steps are most important to monitor closely?
  • Do you want friction metrics compared by state and insurance type?
  • What should happen operationally when the dashboard reveals a problem?
  • Who should have access to aggregate analytics vs. individual application details?

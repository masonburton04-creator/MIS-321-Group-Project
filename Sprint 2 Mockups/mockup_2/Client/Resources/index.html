Metric Definitions & Open Questions

For client review. Nothing in this section is a finalized business rule.

  • Applications started — rows in applications created in the date range.
  • Completion rate — % of started applications that were submitted.
  • Not submitted — 100% minus completion rate. Includes applications still in progress.
  • Awaiting review — applications that are submitted, under review, or escalated.
  • Avg. time to submit — average time from starting an application to submitting it.
  • Funnel — how many applications reached each step (from applications.current_step).
  • Dwell time — average time on a step before finishing it or leaving the site.
  • Exit / quick-exit rate — % of visits to a step that ended with the customer leaving the site (quick = within the threshold below).
  • Validation errors — % of visits that hit at least one form error.
  • Back clicks — times customers pressed Back from that step.

  • A "quick exit" is leaving within 10 seconds. This is a placeholder, not a client-approved definition.
  • "Needs Attention" / "Watch" use a simple score: exit rate + 1.5 × quick-exit rate + 1.2 × validation-error rate. The cut-offs (45 and 25) are placeholders.

  • Employee vs. admin permissions (everyone who signs in can do everything today), password resets, and how staff accounts get created.
  • Sending tracking events from the customer form to analytics_events.
  • Which metrics belong on the landing dashboard vs. deeper drill-down pages.
  • Retention, privacy, and consent requirements for behavioral tracking.
  • Which applicant-level details an agent should be able to open from aggregate analytics.

  • When you say "hover" or "how long users stay," do you mean page time, field-focus time, actual mouse-hover duration, or something else?
  • What should count as leaving "quickly" — a fixed number of seconds, leaving before interacting, or something else?
  • Which application steps are most important to monitor closely?
  • What should happen operationally when the dashboard reveals a problem?
  • Who should have access to aggregate analytics vs. individual application details?
  • What review statuses and actions do your agents actually use?
